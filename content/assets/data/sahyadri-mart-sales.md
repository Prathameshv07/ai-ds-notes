## What it is

Daily sales of **Sahyadri Mart**, a fictional chain of 12 grocery and household stores across Maharashtra: 5 large *Supermarket* stores and 7 small *Express* stores, spread over five regions. The data is **synthetic**: it was generated by a script, not collected from a real company. It is built to behave like real retail data, so practicals on description, distributions, correlation, regression, forecasting, sampling, simulation and ANOVA can all use one file.

## How it was generated

Script: `sahyadri-mart-sales.py` (beside this file), random seed 2026, so every run gives the identical file. The patterns built in, which students can discover:

- **Store size:** Supermarkets have higher footfall and larger bills than Express stores.
- **Growth:** footfall rises about 8% per year.
- **Weekly pattern:** weekends are busiest (Sunday highest), Tuesday lowest.
- **Seasonality:** a dip in July–August (monsoon), a peak in October–November (festive season).
- **Festivals:** footfall jumps on festival days and the days before, most for Diwali; discounts are at least 10% on those days.
- **Rain:** rain falls mainly June–September, heaviest in Mumbai-Konkan, and heavy rain reduces footfall.
- **Marketing levers:** ad spend, discounts and displays each raise footfall a little, with diminishing returns for ad spend; a display works slightly better together with a discount (an interaction effect).
- **Noise:** random day-to-day variation, so relationships are realistic rather than perfect.

**Known imperfection (on purpose):** the rain gauges at S03 (Ratnagiri) and S10 (Nanded) failed on some days, leaving 93 blank `rainfall_mm` values. All other columns are complete.

**Caution for students:** because the data is synthetic, never quote its numbers as facts about real stores or cities.
