### What this job is

An ML engineer turns models into working software. A typical week: train and compare models on a large dataset, write the training pipeline so it can rerun every week, cut a model's response time from 800 ms to 80 ms, package it in a container, and watch how it behaves once real users hit it. Many ML engineers specialise in computer vision or language. The job needs strong software engineering as much as ML theory. In India, ML engineers work at product companies (Flipkart, Swiggy, Meesho, PhonePe), AI startups, banks and fintechs, and the Bengaluru and Hyderabad research and engineering centres of global technology companies. Entry roles: ML Engineer, Software Engineer (ML), Computer Vision Engineer, NLP Engineer; many freshers start as software or data engineers and move across.
