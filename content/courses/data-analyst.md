### What this job is

A data analyst answers business questions with data. A typical request: a manager asks why sales fell in Pune last month. You pull the numbers with SQL, clean them in Excel or pandas, compare stores and weeks, and send back one chart with three lines of explanation. Much of the week also goes into keeping regular reports and dashboards up to date. In India, analysts work at analytics firms (Mu Sigma, Fractal, Tiger Analytics, LatentView), in the analytics teams of IT services companies (TCS, Infosys, Accenture), at consumer apps and fintechs (Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato, PhonePe), in banks, and in the global capability centres of multinationals. Entry roles: Data Analyst, Business Analyst, MIS Executive, Reporting Analyst, Junior BI Analyst.
