### What this job is

A data scientist builds models that predict or explain: which customers will leave, what a flat should cost, which payments look fraudulent. A typical project: turn a vague business question into a measurable target, gather and clean the data, try several models, check honestly how well they work on data they have not seen, and explain the result to the people who will act on it. Engineers usually help put the final model into an app. In India, data scientists work at product companies (Flipkart, Swiggy, Meesho, PhonePe), in banks and fintechs (credit risk, fraud), at analytics firms (Fractal, Tiger Analytics, Mu Sigma), and in the Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune centres of global companies. Freshers usually start as Associate or Junior Data Scientist or ML Analyst, or as a Data Analyst who moves into modelling later.
