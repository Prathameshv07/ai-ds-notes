### What this job is

An AI engineer builds products on top of large language models and other foundation models: a chatbot that answers from a company's documents, an assistant that calls tools, a system that pulls fields out of invoices. A typical week: design prompts and retrieval, connect the model to databases and APIs, build a test set and measure answer quality, cut cost and response time, and guard against wrong or unsafe output. Strong Python and software engineering matter as much as ML theory, and the field changes every few months. In India, AI engineers work at AI startups (such as Sarvam AI), SaaS companies (Freshworks, Zoho), the GenAI practices of IT services firms (TCS, Infosys, Wipro), product companies, and global capability centres. Entry roles: AI Engineer, GenAI Engineer, LLM Application Developer, Software Engineer (AI).
