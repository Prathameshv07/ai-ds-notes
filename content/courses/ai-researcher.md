### What this job is

An AI researcher asks questions nobody has answered yet and tests new methods carefully. A typical month: read recent papers in one area, spot a gap, design an experiment with proper baselines and ablations, run it many times, analyse what went right and wrong, and write it up for a conference, journal or arXiv. The maths runs deeper than in other paths, and patience with failed experiments matters. In India, AI research happens at IISc, the IITs and IIIT Hyderabad, at groups such as AI4Bharat (IIT Madras) and Wadhwani AI, and at industry labs including Microsoft Research India, Google DeepMind (Bengaluru), Adobe Research and TCS Research. Entry routes: Research Assistant or Project Associate on a funded project, Research Engineer, or a PhD place (through GATE, UGC NET JRF or institute entrance exams). Be honest with yourself: most research roles expect an MSc or PhD, so a BSc is the first step.
